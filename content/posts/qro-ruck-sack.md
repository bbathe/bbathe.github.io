I've been doing a lot of POTA, and I got tired of building a different pile of gear every time I left the house. I also travel for work, and I've taken this through airports more than once. So this is the bag I keep packed: a [GORUCK GR1 26L](https://www.goruck.com/products/gr1-usa).

## Radio

The radio is a [Yaesu FT-891](https://www.yaesu.com/product-detail.aspx?Model=FT-891&CatName=HF%20Transceivers/Amplifiers). I want 100 watts to make sure I get an activation no matter how bad conditions are, and the 891 is relatively small and lightweight.

- Yaesu FT-891
- Earbuds
- Hand mic
- Power cord

## Power

Power is the annoying part when you're not at home. The 6 Ah LiFePO4 runs the 891 and is allowed by [TSA](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/whatcanibring/items/lithium-batteries-100-watt-hours-or-less-device). I've got a 12 volt charger for the car and a 120 volt charger for the hotel, plus USB chargers and a battery bank for everything else.

- [6 Ah LiFePO4 battery](https://www.bioennopower.com/products/12v-6ah-lifepo4-battery-pvc)
- 12V LiFePO4 charger
- 120V LiFePO4 charger
- 12V USB charger
- 120V USB charger
- Battery bank
- USB cords

## Vertical

I only carry elements for 20 and 17 meters. I normally operate on 20m, and have 17m available in case there's a contest clogging up the bands. One mast, dedicated elements, 32 radials.

- 20m & 17m elements (16 AWG)
- 32 radials (22 AWG, 16 feet long)
- [BNC To Binding Posts Adapter](https://www.pomonaelectronics.com/products/adapters/coaxial/bnc-m-binding-posts)
- [Feedline Choke](https://www.dxengineering.com/parts/dxe-lpfc-bnc)
- [Carbon 6 mast](https://www.sotabeams.co.uk/carbon-6-ultra-light-6-m-19-6-ft-mast/)
- Mini tripod, camera rod, and 3/4" PVC to shim the rod in the mast
- 25 ft RG-8X coax, UHF to BNC

## Logging and computer

I still take a pad and pens, just in case. The laptop and a Digirig DR-891 are there when I want digital, and a GPS receiver to keep the clock in-sync.

- Pad, pens, pencil
- Laptop
- 120V laptop charger
- [Digirig DR-891](https://digirig.net/product/digirig-dr-891/)
- GPS receiver

## Chair and table

Working off the ground for more than a few minutes is miserable, so the chair & table come with me.

- [Helinox Chair Zero](https://helinox.com/products/chair-zero)
- Chair Zero pad
- [Naceture table](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C6K7FRH5/)

## Miscellaneous

And then all the little stuff that isn't radio but I'd be unhappy without.

- Compactor bag
- Sweat rag
- Aspirin, antacid, salt pills, etc.
- DUDE Wipes
- Headlamp
- Lighter
- Watch
- Readers
- Screwdriver, pliers, and scissors
- Sunglasses
- Hat

I attached a water bottle holder on the GR1, it stays empty until after I get thru TSA.  It's heavy at 23 lbs., everytime I have tried to reduce gear (table, chair, etc.) I've regretted it more than the trouble of lugging it around so it stays.
